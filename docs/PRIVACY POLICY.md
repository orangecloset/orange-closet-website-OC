# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 01 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ORANGE CLOSET handles information when you use the ORANGE CLOSET website ("the Website"). By using the Website, you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the Website.

This policy covers the Website only. It does not cover our physical branches, or conversations on Facebook, Messenger, or other external platforms, which have their own terms and privacy practices.

## 1. No Accounts and No Online Purchases

- The Website is a digital product catalog. It does not offer user accounts or logins, and no purchases are completed through the Website.
- Because of this, we do not collect account credentials, billing, or payment card information from you through the Website.

## 2. Information Stored on Your Device

The Website stores small amounts of data directly in your browser (local storage) so the catalog works well and remembers your choices. This data stays on your device and is not uploaded to us, except where noted:

- **Catalog access:** details that let you view the catalog after unlocking it, so you do not have to re-enter your access details on every visit.
- **Cart and wishlist:** items you have added to your cart or wishlist, so they are kept between visits.
- **Catalog cache and preferences:** saved catalog data and settings that help pages load faster.
- **Scroll position:** your place on a page during the same visit, so back and forward navigation feels natural.

You can clear this data at any time by clearing your browser's site data or, if you no longer want access, by removing access to the catalog.

## 3. Information We Automatically Receive

- When you visit the Website, standard technical information is sent to us by the services that host and operate it (such as Vercel, our hosting provider). This includes things like your device and browser type, your IP address, and the pages you view.
- We use this information to keep the Website working and secure, and to understand how the catalog is used through an analytics service so we can improve it.
- Analytics data is aggregated and anonymized where possible, and we do not use it to identify you.

## 4. No Cookies for Advertising

- The Website does not use cookies for advertising or for tracking you across other websites.
- Our analytics service is cookieless and does not follow you around the internet.

## 5. What We Do With Your Information

We use the information described above only to:

- operate, maintain, and secure the Website;
- verify and manage access to the catalog;
- understand how the catalog is used in order to improve it;
- respond if you choose to contact us (for example, by email, as stated in our Terms and Conditions);
- comply with applicable laws.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

## 6. Who We Share It With

- **Service providers:** we share information only with the service providers that help us run and host the Website, such as our hosting provider (Vercel), and the database and image-storage providers that deliver the catalog. They process information on our behalf and only to the extent needed to provide their services.
- **Legal reasons:** we may disclose information if required by law, or to protect our rights, safety, or property.

## 7. Third-Party Links

The Website links to external services, including Facebook, Messenger, and social profiles. We do not control them and are not responsible for their content or privacy practices. Their own privacy policies apply.

## 8. How Long We Keep Information

- Data stored on your device stays there until you clear it.
- We keep the limited technical and analytics information described above only as long as needed for the purposes in this policy, or as required by law.

## 9. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the Website and the limited information it handles, including restricting access to the catalog. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 10. Your Rights

If you are in the Philippines, you have rights under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), including the right to be informed, to access, to correct, and to object to the processing of your personal information, as applicable. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.
